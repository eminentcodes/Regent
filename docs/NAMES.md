# Name ideas

## How to judge a name

- one word, and easy to spell after only hearing it
- means something without needing an explanation
- not already a large product
- works in an article title and as a GitHub repo name

## The strongest picks

**Cairn** - a cairn is a stack of stones that travellers add to as they pass. It is literally collective memory: each visitor leaves a marker and the next one benefits. That is our two-tier design in a single image, and the cold northern stone motif sits nicely beside Walrus.

Tagline: every conversation leaves a stone.

**Engram** - the engram is the physical trace a memory leaves in the brain. Scientific, precise, unique, and it sounds like infrastructure rather than a toy.

Tagline: memory with a physical trace.

**The Usual** - the warm one. A shop bot that remembers you, and "the usual?" is exactly the moment we are trying to create. Any business owner understands it instantly with no explanation.

Tagline: it already knows the usual.

**Otter** - sea otters raft together and hold hands so they do not drift apart. That is memory that follows you across devices, as a picture. Friendly, warm, memorable.

Tagline: never drift apart.

**Morsa** - walrus in Spanish, Portuguese and Italian. Short, brandable, and a knowing nod to the platform without copying it.

Tagline: the memory your customers keep.
## More candidates

Collective memory and craft

- Loom - weaving threads of memory together
- Vellum - the surface that keeps whatever is written on it
- Quipu - the Inca knot record, an ancient memory technology
- Saga - your story, accumulated over time
- Yarn - stories, and thread
- Ledger - the record of what actually happened

Memory science

- Mneme - one of the Muses, memory itself
- Mnemosyne - the fuller name, goddess of memory
- Anamnesis - recollection, the act of calling something back
- Hippocampus - the brain region that forms memory, and a fun animal
- Retain - plain but very clear
- Recollect - formal, a little stiff

Sea and north, sitting next to Walrus

- Narwhal - the walrus cousin, playful and recognisable
- Tusk - short, strong, slightly aggressive
- Reef - collective, built up slowly by many
- Walross and Hvalross - walrus in German and Norwegian, less brandable

Shop and service, warm

- Regular - the customer who keeps coming back
- Concierge - classic service memory
- Maitre - the one who remembers your table
- Frontdesk - plain and useful
- Shopkeep - homely and friendly

## Risks to check before committing

- GitHub org and repo name availability
- domain availability on .xyz or .app
- trademark clash in the AI or retail space
- easy to say out loud on a video call
- whether the article title reads well with it

## Top three

1. **Cairn** - best meaning, and the best fit for the shared collective tier
2. **Engram** - best if we want to sound technical and infrastructural
3. **The Usual** - best if we want to sound human and commercial
