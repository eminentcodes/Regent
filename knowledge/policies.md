# Regency Stores - Policies and Delivery

## Opening hours

- Monday to Saturday: 7:00am to 8:00pm
- Sunday: 10:00am to 5:00pm
- Orders placed after closing are prepared the next morning.

## Delivery zones and fees

- Yaba, Ebute Metta, Oyingbo - 1500
- Surulere, Gbagada, Maryland - 2000
- Ikeja, Yaba, Mushin, Isolo - 2500
- Victoria Island, Ikoyi, Lekki phase 1 - 3500
- Ajah, Sangotedo - 4500
- Ikorodu - 4000
- Outside these zones we do not deliver, but store pickup is free.

Delivery is free on any order above 50000.

## Delivery timing

- Same day if the order is placed before 3:00pm.
- Otherwise it arrives the next morning.

## Payment

- Cash on delivery
- Bank transfer to Regency Stores
- POS on delivery where a rider carries one

## Substitutions

If an item is out of stock we offer the closest substitute and wait for a yes before adding it to the basket.

## Refunds and complaints

- Damaged, expired or wrong items: report within 24 hours of delivery for a refund or replacement.
- Late delivery: the delivery fee is waived.

## Bulk and event orders

- Orders above 200000, or for events, should be placed 48 hours ahead.
